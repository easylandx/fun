Field Notes

Same shrug, different target.

Origin

IDGAF

A general-purpose attitude that's circulated through slang for decades, long before the internet gave it an acronym. It exploded as shorthand in texting and social captions in the 2010s, then got its own pop-culture spike once artists started building entire songs and hooks around the phrase.

Origin

IDFWU

More specific and more recent. It rose fast in hip-hop and R&B vocabulary in the mid-2010s, largely thanks to a breakout single that used the phrase as its title and hook, cementing it as the go-to way to announce you're done with a specific person.

What it actually means

Untargeted

IDGAF is a stance, not a message to anyone in particular. It describes how you're choosing to move through a situation — unbothered, unfazed, opting out of caring about outcomes or opinions.

What it actually means

Targeted

IDFWU is relational. It's a statement about one specific person or group — a declaration that you're cutting contact, trust, or loyalty. It's less "I feel free" and more "we're done here."
