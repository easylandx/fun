About the Bureau

Founded the moment someone asked whether a fly that loses its wings is now called a walk.

The government realized these questions could no longer be ignored. Today, thousands of highly trained Overthinking Specialists work around the clock investigating life's least urgent mysteries.

Our mission is simple: to answer the questions that appear in your head at 2:13 AM — and refuse to leave until they've been given an official case number.
