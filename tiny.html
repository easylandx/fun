FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Isn't this a bit ridiculous?+
Yes. So is pretending only giant achievements matter.
What counts as a Tiny Victory?+
If you almost didn't do it... but did anyway. It counts.
Can I nominate someone else?+
Absolutely. Parents, teachers, coworkers, and people who refill the office coffee machine are frequently nominated.
Can I celebrate the same victory every day?+
Please do. Consistency deserves trophies too.
Do I need proof?+
No. We operate on the honor system. Mostly because nobody has time to investigate whether you really drank eight glasses of water.
